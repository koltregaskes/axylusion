About this site

Privacy

Axy Lusion is a static portfolio. Here is what loads while you browse and what happens when you follow a link.

Pages, fonts and images

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, with Cloudflare used for DNS. Fonts load directly from Google Fonts at fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Gallery images load directly from the Cloudflare R2 public media host pub-7bea5d72ad544bb685e1335d2e6bdd10.r2.dev. Your browser connects to those providers to request these files.

Provider logging settings and retention periods have not been confirmed, so this notice makes no promise about them.

External links and media

Links lead to Kol's other websites, social profiles, creative tools, news sources and ElevenLabs music listings and licence pages. These include X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Midjourney, Suno and GitHub. When you follow a link, the destination's privacy practices apply.

There are no embedded social feeds, audio players or video players on the current pages. Music and video links take you to the external provider.

No analytics or newsletter yet

No analytics or newsletter yet: the site currently has no analytics script, newsletter sign-up form or subscription service. Gallery search and news filters run in your browser. This notice will need updating before analytics, subscriptions or embedded players are added.
