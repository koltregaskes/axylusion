Privacy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

Who runs Axy Lusion

I am Kol Tregaskes. I run this creative portfolio as an individual and am the controller responsible for personal information I handle through it. For a privacy question or request, use the contact links on the About page, including X or Instagram. There is no verified site email address published yet. Contacting me on a social platform also involves that platform.

What happens when you browse

The current site has no forms, accounts, analytics or newsletter sign-up. Its scripts set no cookies and use neither localStorage nor sessionStorage. Gallery search and news filters stay in page memory and reset when you reload. Your browser may cache files in the usual way.

Pages are served directly by GitHub Pages. Fonts and some images come from the providers below. Each request gives the provider your IP address, user agent (browser details), the requested file and the time of the request. A referrer may also be sent, depending on your browser and referrer policy; cross-site requests from pages using this site's strict-origin-when-cross-origin policy normally send only the site's origin, not the full page address.

  • GitHub Pages (axylusion.com). Delivers this site's pages and files. GitHub receives standard request logs, including your IP address and request details. Privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com). Delivers the stylesheet and font files used by these pages. Privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare R2 (pub-7bea5d72ad544bb685e1335d2e6bdd10.r2.dev). Delivers gallery and homepage images when your browser loads them. Privacy policy.

I use these services to display and protect the portfolio, relying on my legitimate interests in running a working, secure website. Provider logs are not an analytics dashboard operated by this site. Their retention settings have not been confirmed. Providers may process information outside the UK; their policies explain their own practices, retention and transfer arrangements. I do not promise a particular retention period or UK-only processing.

Cloudflare provides DNS and the R2 image service. A Cloudflare proxy for page traffic is only proposed and awaits Kol's approval; it is not the current page-serving setup.

External links and media

Links lead to Kol's other websites, social profiles, creative tools, news sources and ElevenLabs music listings and licence pages. These include X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Midjourney, Suno and GitHub. When you follow a link, the destination's privacy practices apply.

There are no embedded social feeds, audio players or video players on the current pages. Music and video links take you to the external provider.

Your rights and complaints

Under UK GDPR, you can ask for access to personal information held about you and for inaccurate information to be corrected. Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for deletion or restriction, object to processing (including processing based on legitimate interests), or request portability. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it. These rights have conditions and do not all apply to every type of processing.

Use the About page contact routes above to make a request. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator.
